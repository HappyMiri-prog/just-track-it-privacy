Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Just Track It is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. The application operates as an offline-first utility, ensuring your personal habits and daily activity remain strictly on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

Just Track It does not collect, transmit, track, or sell any personal information. There are no third-party tracking frameworks, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers (IDFA/IDFV) integrated into the application.

2. Local-Only Storage

All user entries, habit frequencies, streaks, and application settings are persisted locally on your device within isolated sandbox storage (NSDocumentDirectory). This data is never transferred to external servers maintained by us.

3. External Services and Links

The application includes an optional link to subscribe to our product newsletter via a native in-app browser view (SFSafariViewController). Any information submitted through that external subscription form is handled directly and exclusively by the newsletter service provider in accordance with their privacy policy.

4. Data Deletion and Backup

Because your data is stored solely on your hardware, deleting the application completely removes all stored trackers and historical logs from your device, unless retained in your private Apple iCloud or local iTunes backups.
