Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Just Track It is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. It is an offline-first app: your daily entries stay on your device and, if you keep iCloud backup turned on, in your own personal iCloud account. We have no servers of our own and never see your data.

1. We Do Not Collect Your Data

We do not collect, receive, track, or sell any personal information. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no advertising frameworks, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA). The only data that leaves your device is the backup sent to your own iCloud account (see section 3) and anything you explicitly choose to export or share.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

Your entries (daily scores, moods, activities, habits, custom trackers and notes), goals, streaks and app settings (such as your chosen display name, language, gender preference for wording, color palette and reminder time) are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. The app also keeps an automatic local copy of your data on the device every 7 days, so you can recover it if something goes wrong. None of this is ever sent to servers maintained by us.

3. iCloud Backup

iCloud backup is turned on by default so that you can recover your data if you reinstall the app or change iPhones. Your backup is stored only in your own private iCloud account, in the app's iCloud container, and travels directly between your device and Apple's iCloud service. We never receive, store, or have access to it.

  • iCloud backup is a backup of this iPhone, not a live sync between devices.
  • You can turn it off at any time in the app under Settings → Backup. Turning it off stops new backups but does not delete copies already stored in your iCloud.
  • You can delete the stored copy at any time from your iPhone's Settings, in the iCloud storage section for your Apple Account, by managing the storage used by Just Track It.

4. Daily Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push server is involved, and the reminder text does not include your entries or notes. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Just Track It may offer optional subscriptions and a one-time purchase, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, process, or store any payment or billing information; that is handled exclusively by Apple in accordance with its own privacy practices.

6. Export and Sharing

You can export a backup file or share an image (for example, your annual summary). These actions only happen when you choose them, using the standard iOS share sheet. Once exported or shared, the file is handled by the destination you choose.

7. External Services and Links

The app includes optional links to this policy and to subscribe to our newsletter, which open in a browser view. Any information you submit through the newsletter form is handled by the newsletter service provider in accordance with its own privacy policy.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • In the app: Settings → Reset app and delete data permanently deletes all your entries, settings and automatic local copies from this iPhone. Your iCloud backup is kept, so you can restore it when you set up the app again.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Your iCloud backup remains in your iCloud account until you delete it from your iPhone's iCloud storage settings (see section 3). Data may also remain in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or computer backups) until those are replaced or deleted.

9. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
